# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include harassment, public or private
abuse, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through the private route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) with the subject `Code of Conduct`. If that route
is unavailable, ask a maintainer for a private contact route without posting
the report or personal information publicly. The maintainers
will review a report, protect the reporter's confidentiality where possible,
and take the action they consider appropriate, including removing content or
restricting participation.

## Scope

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, Discussions and other
community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.
Maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards
fairly, including when a report concerns another maintainer.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers consider the impact, context and recurrence of a violation:

1. **Correction:** explain the harm and request a change in behaviour.
2. **Warning:** set explicit boundaries for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** suspend participation for a stated period after
   serious or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent restriction:** exclude people whose severe or persistent
   conduct makes participation unsafe for others.

Reports are reviewed confidentially where possible. Restrictions and any
conditions for returning should be communicated privately to the person
involved; reporters' identities and private details must not be published.
